English

Lucky Bright Lotto is a number-reference app that works without an account. This policy is effective 2026-11-25.

Personal data we collect

The app does not collect personal data and does not transmit personal data. It does not receive your name, email address, phone number, location, advertising ID, device identifier, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Accounts, ads, and analytics

  • There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account.
  • There are no ads.
  • There are no analytics tools and no tracking SDKs.

Data that stays on the device

The following stays only on the phone. It is not sent to the developer’s servers.

  • Saved number combinations: an on-device SQLite database
  • Theme (light, dark, or system): SharedPreferences
  • Cached public draw results: the same on-device SQLite database. These are published lottery results, not personal data.

Uninstalling the app deletes the saved combinations, theme, and cache from the device.

Network and permissions

The only permission the app requests is INTERNET.

The app makes a network request only when you refresh. Tapping “데이터 최신화” (refresh) on the home screen, or pulling to refresh on the home or statistics screen, fetches public draw results over HTTPS.

  • Destination: the public draw results published by 동행복권 (Donghang Lottery, www.dhlottery.co.kr)
  • What is requested: public winning numbers for lottery rounds. The request does not include information that identifies you.
  • Opening the app does not contact the network, and the app does not upload data in the background.

That optional fetch is the only network request the app makes.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertising or analytics companies. The only outside connection is the optional refresh of public draw results from 동행복권.

Affiliation, ticket sales, and winning

Lucky Bright Lotto is not affiliated with 동행복권 (www.dhlottery.co.kr) or the Korea Lottery Commission. It is not an official app, and it does not sell lottery tickets. Numbers shown in the app are for entertainment and reference. There is no guarantee of winning.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy: kkarare@gmail.com. If the policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
